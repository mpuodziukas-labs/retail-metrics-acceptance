## 1. Move first-time buyers to a second order

What the data shows: 1,368 of 2,808 identified customers (48.72%) ordered more than once. Those repeat customers account for 79.30% of identified-customer revenue. Only 13.24% of a cohort orders again in the month after its first order.

Recommendation: target the first-month window with a second-order offer or reminder, and track month-1 retention as the success measure.

What the data shows: 15.40% of revenue comes from lines with no customer ID, so none of the repeat or cohort metrics above can see it.

Recommendation: ask for an identifier at checkout so the unattributed share shrinks, then re-run this pipeline and compare the share.

What the data shows: 2011-11 is the highest revenue month at 36,327.03, which is 13.56% of the period total. Its invoice cancellation rate is 2.60%, against 2.86% over the whole period.

Recommendation: plan stock and support capacity ahead of 2011-11, and review cancellations in that month first.
